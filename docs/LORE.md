# Lore: canon vs. this game

*Soul Eater* is by Atsushi Ohkubo. This is a fan-made game. To respect the source, every origin and system separates **what the series establishes** from **what this game invents**. The same text appears in-game in the Race Index's *Lore* tab (`Config/LoreCodex.luau`).

Ground rules used throughout:

- **Origins vs. roles.** Rollable origins are not all biological species: zombies and mummies are undead, a Golem is a soul bound to a built body, Sorcerer is a practice, and Shinigami is a lineage. *Meister* is a combat role, never a race.
- **No playable canon characters.** Players never become Medusa, Eibon, Kid, Asura and so on. Schools and abilities are *inspired by* canon motifs.
- **Powerful canon states are progression, not rolls.** Kishin is an endgame questline with a temporary, risky transformation. Black Blood is a quest-unlocked status. Death Scythe appears only as a cosmetic title.
- **Original mechanics are labelled as original.**
- **Weapons are named honestly.** Where a weapon in the reference art does not match a canon wielder, the inventory says so (see *Weapon notes*).

### Humans
*Rollable origin (biological race)*

| Canon (Soul Eater) | This game (original interpretation) |
|---|---|
| Most students at the DWMA (Shibusen) are human Meisters. | Humans gain bonus XP, mastery and perception to reward versatility. |
| Soul Perception is a trained skill, not a racial trait; Maka is singled out for her skill at it. | Adaptive Focus is an original ability earned at mastery Stage II. |

### Demon Weapons
*Rollable origin (human lineage with transformation)*

| Canon (Soul Eater) | This game (original interpretation) |
|---|---|
| Demon Weapons are humans who can transform their bodies into weapons. | Demon Weapon is an origin; Meister is a separate combat role. A Demon Weapon cannot take the Meister role. |
| A Demon Weapon becomes a Death Scythe by consuming 99 Kishin Eggs and one witch's soul. | Weapon Resonance and Resonance Surge are original mechanics representing synchronization. |
| Weapons fight alongside Meisters; matching soul wavelengths (Soul Resonance) multiplies their power. | Death Scythe is used only as a cosmetic Stage III title, not as a power level. |
| Partial transformation (e.g. a blade emerging from an arm) appears in the series. |  |

### Werewolves

### Witches
*Rollable origin (magic-born race)*

| Canon (Soul Eater) | This game (original interpretation) |
|---|---|
| Witches are magic users; their magic commonly carries an animal motif (Medusa's snakes, Eruka's frogs). | Witch schools are inspired by canon witches; players learn a school and never become those characters. |
| Witches can hide their souls from Soul Perception (Soul Protect). | Soul Protect is modeled as being hidden from non-Shinigami Soul Perception. |
| Witch souls are normally driven toward destruction; Kim Diehl is notable for regenerative magic. |  |
| Kim Diehl is both a witch and a Meister (partnered with Jacqueline), so witches can wield weapons. |  |

### Sorcerers
*Rollable origin (scholarly tradition, not a species)*

| Canon (Soul Eater) | This game (original interpretation) |
|---|---|
| Eibon, one of the Great Old Ones, is described as a sorcerer who created many Demon Tools (including BREW). | Player sorcerers are human practitioners of an original sorcery tradition, not Eibon or his kin. |
|  | Ember, Void and Artifice schools are original; Artifice is inspired by Eibon's Demon Tools. |

### Shinigami
*Rollable origin (divine lineage) with mastery-gated power*

| Canon (Soul Eater) | This game (original interpretation) |
|---|---|
| Lord Death (Shinigami-sama) leads the DWMA; Death the Kid is his son. | Player Shinigami are an original lineage and start modest; mastery unlocks the Lines of Sanzu cosmetic and stronger Death's Authority variants. |
| Kid's Lines of Sanzu connect as his power as a Shinigami matures. | Divine Soul Perception revealing soul-protected enemies is an original mechanic. |

### Golems
*Rollable origin (soul-bound construct)*

| Canon (Soul Eater) | This game (original interpretation) |
|---|---|
| The Oldest Golem near Loew Village was built about 800 years ago and is occupied by a soul. | A Golem origin is a soul bound to a crafted body — not a biological race. |
|  | Living Fortress and Colossus Slam are original; its slow, visible wind-ups are its counterplay. |

### Kishin
*Endgame questline + temporary transformation (never a roll)*

| Canon (Soul Eater) | This game (original interpretation) |
|---|---|
| A Kishin is born when a being devours innocent human souls; Asura was the first. | Kishin Awakening is an original corruption state unlocked by the endgame questline and fueled by Madness. |
| Kishin Eggs are the souls of humans corrupted by eating innocent souls. | It drains your health and resets your Madness, so it is a risky burst, not a permanent form. |
| Asura's madness is tied to fear. |  |

### Black Blood
*Special progression / status system (not a race)*

| Canon (Soul Eater) | This game (original interpretation) |
|---|---|
| Black Blood was created by the witch Medusa; Crona and Ragnarok carry it. | Black Blood is unlocked through a quest and grants a defensive hardening reaction at low health, at the cost of Madness. |
| It can harden to block attacks and is associated with madness; Soul is infected during the series. |  |

### Madness Wavelengths
*Combat & encounter mechanic*

| Canon (Soul Eater) | This game (original interpretation) |
|---|---|
| Madness spreads through wavelengths; the Kishin's madness can infect others. | Madness Wraith enemies emit wavelengths that raise your Madness meter. High Madness raises damage dealt and taken. |

### Soul Wavelength Compatibility
*Partner synchronization mechanic*

| Canon (Soul Eater) | This game (original interpretation) |
|---|---|
| Meisters and weapons must match soul wavelengths to resonate; some pairs are incompatible. | Each student picks a wavelength type at creation. A Demon Weapon and a Meister can synchronize; compatibility scales the bonus. |

### Meisters
*Combat role*

| Canon (Soul Eater) | This game (original interpretation) |
|---|---|
| Meisters wield Demon Weapons and are trained at the DWMA. | Meister is a role any non-weapon origin can pick. It improves weapon mastery and enables synchronization. |
